About this game
arrow_forward
Step onto the pitch and become the hero of every moment in Soccer Super Star!
Swipe, curve, and score through 5000+ levels of fast, skill-based soccer action. This is your road to 2026 glory on the world stage as you chase the ultimate cup.
No energy limits. No waiting. No pressure.
Just pure soccer, played your way anytime and anywhere, even offline.
Easy to pick up and challenging to master, every goal is in your hands.
With simple flick controls and realistic ball physics, you can draw passes, bend shots, and curve the ball exactly where you want.
Sounds easy? As you progress, you’ll need precision and creativity to unlock defenses and find the perfect path to goal.
Every level is a smart, rewarding challenge where placement and control make the difference.
Experience dynamic gameplay across beautifully designed pitches and evolving match conditions.
From clean finishes to complex attacking plays, every scenario feels fresh and satisfying.
Take on thousands of handcrafted levels and rise through your journey toward football glory.
Build your path, chase the moment, and make every shot count to get one step closer to lifting the cup on the world arena.
Discover new ways to play with special modes and unique challenges that put you in key moments.
Take decisive shots, complete crucial plays, and step into goalkeeping situations when it matters most.
Enjoy smooth, responsive gameplay powered by improved AI and fluid match flow.
Every pass, every curve, and every goal feels natural and satisfying.
Play anytime and anywhere, even offline.
Whether you have a quick break or time for a longer session, the pitch is always ready.
Download now and start your journey.
The next unforgettable goal is just one swipe away.
