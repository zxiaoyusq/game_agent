About 29 Card Game
Card Game 29 is a famous round-winning (or trick-taking) card game played with a deck of 32 cards. It is a descendant of the Jass family of games from the Netherlands and brought to the subcontinent in the 18th Century by Dutch traders.
Similar games: 28 card game, Fifty Six, 304, Thunee, Thuni
Key Features of 29 Card Game
It is played with J, 9, A, 10, K, Q, 8, 7 from each suit. Hence there are 32 cards in the game.
The points of each card are: J (3 points), 9 (2), A (1), 10 (1) and K, Q, 8, 7 have 0 points each.
There are 3+2+1+1 = 7 points in each suit, hence a total of 7 x 4 = 28 points. There is an optional additional point for winning the last hand, taking the total to 29.
👥 The game is played by four players in two teams (alternate players form a team), with each player receiving a hand of 8 cards.
🏆 The aim of the game is to win rounds (or tricks) and score points, with the highest card in each round winning all four cards of that round. The objective is to win a set by reaching 6 red points (or pushing the opponents to 6 black points).
Experience the excitement of the top card game 29, on your mobile device! Play with friends or challenge the AI in this fast-paced trick-taking game. Bid for the trump suit, strategise your moves and win tricks to score points. With intuitive controls and smooth gameplay, 29 is easy to learn but hard to master.
Discover the thrill of the 29 card game free online!
How to play?
Engage in exciting gameplay as you challenge your skills against the AI in offline mode or connect with friends and relatives from anywhere in the world in the 29 card game online mode. It is the best card game for excellent brain exercise, a puzzle for short, fast-paced games that require a mix of strategy, skill, and partner chemistry to win
Offline game
Click on the ‘Play Offline’ button to jump into the offline game mode against AI.
Bid the points between 16 and 29.
If you win the bid, set the trump card to bias the game towards your team.
In each round, the highest card of the first player’s suit wins all the cards, unless another player has trumped it.
Play all the eight rounds and try to win the points you bid for.
Play on multiple games to get +6 in the set score card to win the set.
Online game
Click on the ‘Play Online’ button to create or join a multiplayer game with friends.
If you are creating the game, share the joining link with other friends using other Apps like WhatsApp or Messenger.
If you are joining the game, join using the link received from a friend and then select a seat. The host can add bots if atleast one other player has joined.
When all four players have joined, the game will start for all players.
Play on with your friends to bid, win games and win sets!
Why choose 29 Card Game?
🔢 In our game, you will find Double, Re-Double, Pair, Single Hand, 7th Card Trump, Joker Trump and all the common rules implemented.
