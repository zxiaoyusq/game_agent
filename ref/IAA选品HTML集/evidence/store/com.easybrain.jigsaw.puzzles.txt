About this game
arrow_forward
Get an engaging experience with jigsaw puzzles in one of the world’s most-downloaded puzzle games from a top developer!
Jigsaw Puzzles is an addictive, easy-to-play game with over 30,000 FREE cute HD pictures. Keep your favorite puzzles at hand and enjoy them even when you're offline. No points, no gimmicks, and no missing pieces, simply enjoy hours of fun putting jigsaw puzzles together to calm down and relax.
Created by game experts and played by millions of puzzle lovers worldwide, Jigsaw Puzzles helps you exercise your short-term memory and concentration, while passing the time with real jigsaw puzzles and without taking up as much room.
Indulge yourself and plunge into the wonderful world of offline jigsaw puzzle games for seniors! Enjoy piecing new bright magic jigsaw puzzles together every day!
Our free puzzle game for adults includes a large number of beautiful images of different difficulty puzzles from easy to hard. The game difficulty is based on the number of jigsaw puzzle pieces, which should be put together.
A free jigsaw puzzle app on your device encompasses a wide range of HD pictures and is as challenging and attractive as real puzzles. If you are excited about playing offline puzzle games, you will definitely love our Jigsaw Puzzles for adults!
Being a stress relieving and relaxing offline game, daily jigsaw puzzles for adults will help you to take a break and get rid of the everyday routine.
Discover the colorful world of jigsaw puzzles for free!
Features:
• Tons of jigsaw puzzles for free with beautiful, high quality pictures. Choose from a variety of categories such as Colors, Flowers, Nature, Animals, Art, Landmarks, Hard picture puzzles and others
• Daily free puzzle games for seniors. Get a new HD jigsaw puzzle every day and try to solve it
• Coins for exclusive content. Get coins by completing HD jigsaw puzzles. Spend them to get exclusive puzzles and collections!
• Mystery jigsaw puzzles for seniors. Challenge yourself to reveal what is hidden in a picture
• Daily updated gallery. You’ll never run out of free puzzles in our jigsaw box
• Offline games. Download puzzle pictures and play without internet
• Helpful hints. Use Hint to match the next piece to the picture puzzle if you are stuck
• From 36 to 400 puzzle pieces. The more pieces, the harder your jigsaw puzzle games become
• Custom backgrounds. Choose your favourite appearance to solve free jigsaw puzzle games for seniors with more pleasure.
Make every day relaxing and entertaining putting jigsaw puzzle pieces together! Enjoy our free puzzle games!
