About this game
arrow_forward
Yalla Ludo is a vibrant app that fuses classic board games—Ludo, Jackaroo, and Domino—with real-time voice chat! Whether you're in the mood for gameplay or hoping to connect with new friends in a lively voice chat room, Yalla Ludo delivers a truly immersive experience.
😃 [Voice Chat with Friends]
Enjoy real-time voice chat that lets you share tips and tricks with fellow gamers anytime, anywhere. Make new friends while having fun! Add personality to your gameplay with expressive voice chat.
🎲 [Various Game Modes]
Ludo: Choose between 2&4 Players mode and Team mode. Each mode has 4 gameplays: Classic, Master, Quick, and Arrow. You can also use Magic tools to make the game more fun!
Domino: Play in 2&4 Players mode, each featuring two gameplays: Draw Game and All Five.
Others: More new games are waiting for you.
🎮 [Brand New Jackaroo]
Get ready for fast-paced Jackaroo gameplay! Pick from a variety of game modes (Basic, Complex, and Quick), and team up with friends to claim victory. Make your moves even more fun by expressing yourself with lively game stickers!
🎙️ [Voice Chat Room]
Join the global public chat room, where you can connect with people from all over the world. Chat freely, share ideas, and send cute gifts! Broaden your network of friends and enjoy a relaxed time.
🎁 [Generous Rewards Await You]
Yalla Ludo offers multiple daily activities. Complete game or chat room tasks to earn various rewards (such as golds, diamonds, skin fragments, and gifts, etc). With daily tasks and arrival chests, you'll always find something exciting!
Yalla Ludo connects you with others, so let's enjoy the joyful moments in Yalla Ludo!
Subscribe to Yalla Ludo VIP to enjoy the extra advanced features:
Collect free daily golds, diamonds, and VIP daily benefits.
Privileged game room: create your room in the VIP room, invite others to play together.
-----------------------------------
Ludo VIP includes two types: Knight and Baron. The price for Knight is USD 11.99/month and the price for Baron is USD 39.99/month. Prices are in U.S. dollars, may vary in countries other than the U.S. and are subject to change without notice.
No cancellation of the current subscription is allowed during the active subscription period. You could still have great fun in Yalla Ludo without becoming a Yalla Ludo VIP.
We will continue to give our best shot to provide you with more fun games which will enrich your daily life.

Terms of Service: https://www.yallaludo.com/term/EN/TermOfService.html#TermsOfService
