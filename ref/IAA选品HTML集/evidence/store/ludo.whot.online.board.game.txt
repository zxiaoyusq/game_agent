About this game
arrow_forward
Get ready for the super fun with Whot! Ludo Party, the all-in-one board game extravaganza! This amazing game brings together three fantastic games you know and love: Ludo, Bead, and the exciting classic card game of Whot! Perfect for playing with friends and family, both online and offline!
🎲 Ludo Fun for Everyone! 🎲
Relive the classic board game of Ludo with multiple exciting ways to play!
Quick Mode: For fast-paced, thrilling matches when you're short on time.
Classic Mode: Enjoy the traditional Ludo experience you remember.
2-Players & 4-Players: Challenge your friends and family in exciting head-to-head or four-way battles.
Play with Computer: Test your skills against smart AI opponents.
Local Multiplayer: Gather your loved ones for fun-filled Ludo sessions with friends and family on a single device.
🃏 Whot! The King of Cards! 🃏
Experience the thrill of Whot!, the beloved classic card game! Outsmart your opponents and be the first to empty your hand.
Play with Computer: Sharpen your Whot! skills against challenging AI.
Online Multiplayer: Connect with online friends and players from around the world for exciting Whot! matches.
Local Multiplayer: Enjoy exciting Whot! games with your friends and family in local mode.
⚪⚫ Bead Game Challenge! ⚫⚪
Dive into the strategic world of the Bead game! Choose between two exciting variations:
Bead 12: A fast-paced and engaging version.
Bead 16: A more complex and strategic challenge.
Play with Computer: Test your Bead game prowess against intelligent AI.
Local Multiplayer: Enjoy the Bead game with your friends and family on one device.
Whot! Ludo Party is the perfect game for multiplayers of all ages! Whether you're looking for a nostalgic board game experience, the excitement of a classic card game, or a strategic bead challenge, this game has it all. Connect online with friends, gather your family for local multiplayer fun, or challenge the computer – the possibilities are endless! Download Whot! Ludo Party now and let the games begin!
