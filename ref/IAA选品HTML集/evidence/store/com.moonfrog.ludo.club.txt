Ludo is a modern version of the game played by Indian kings and queens in ancient times; popularly during the Mahabharat it was called Chaupar. All you need to play this game is roll the dice and move your tokens to reach the centre of board. Defeat other players, become the King of Ludo.
Features:
* Play against the computer and practice when there is no internet connection
* Play with your family and friends through local and online multiplayer.
* Play 2 to 4 players in local multiplayer mode.
* Invite and challenge your Facebook friends.
* Play with global players and make new friends.
* Private chat with your Facebook friends and game friends.
* You don’t need to sacrifice your data to become a ludo star. This super ludo online game uses very little data and runs very smoothly on 2G, 3G, 4G!
Ludo Club is a very addictive game that you can play with your family and friends and enjoy it. You will play this Ludo for hours and it will be fun for the whole family. This is as easy as playing tic tac toe.
Ludo has many names in different parts of the world like Fia, Fia-spel (Fia the game), Le Jeu de Dada (The Game of Dada), Non t'arrabbiare, Fia med knuff (fia with push), Cờ cá nwày, Uckers , Griniaris, Petits Chevaux (little horses), Ki nevet a în în, برسي (Berzis/Berzis). People also misspell Ludo as Lodu, Chakka, Lido, Lado, Ledo, Leto, Lido, Lado, Lodo, Loodo and Lodo Game.
