Play multiplayer matches with your friends. Carrom Pool is a physics based online game that can be played by upto four players. Challenge and compete with players from around the world in the best board game.
Enjoy various Carrom game modes in both offline and online mode. You can play with your friends on the same device. Get matched with players of similar skill and have a fun.
Customise your pieces with a huge variety of unlock-able items! Show off your style to players from all over the world!

► Weekly new time-limited events that will keep you hooked. Play more to win more.
