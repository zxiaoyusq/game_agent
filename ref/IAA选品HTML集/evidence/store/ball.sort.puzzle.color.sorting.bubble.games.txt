About this game
arrow_forward
As the most relaxing and addictive color sorting game, this ball puzzle is engineered to both entertain and sharpen your cognitive faculties.
While strategically sorting the colored balls to fill each bottle with the same color, the Zen-like focus it cultivates will relieve stress and provide a necessary distraction from daily routines.
This classic color sorting game is intuitive to play, but difficult to master. Tap to transfer a colored ball from one bottle and stack it into another. Success requires careful strategic moves and advanced planning skills. With thousands of challenging ball sort puzzles available, each move demands foresight. One misstep can lead to an inescapable block! This Ball Sort game is the best mental exercise for you to train logical thinking and maintain mental agility.
KEY FEATURES
🆓 Absolutely FREE sorting game for adults
➡️ Intuitive control, just tap to sort the ball
✅ Thousands of levels to challenge, varying difficulty & infinite joy
⏳ NO TIMER, enjoy the Ball Sort puzzles at your own Zen pace
▶️ NO penalties, you can restart your current level at any time
💡 Use "Undo" to go back to previous steps, or click "Add" to add an extra bottle
🧠 Sharpen your mind in relaxing games
🎮 Strategic but highly addictive gameplay
📶 OFFLINE game, no need for network connection
👤 Designed for adults and logical thinkers
HOW TO PLAY
- Tap any bottle to pick up the top ball, then tap another bottle to move the ball into it.
- You can only stack the ball into a bottle with the same color ball on top and enough space.
- The level is conquered when balls of the same color are sorted into a single bottle.
- Each bottle can only be placed with 4 balls.
- Use "Undo" to go back to previous steps.
- Add an extra bottle if you get stuck.
- You can restart the current level at any time.
This challenging yet mindful Ball Sort game offers sustained strategic engagement through its color sorting puzzles. Share this brain training tool with fellow adults and enjoy a focused, relaxing experience of sorting games.
Engage your strategic mind in this ball color matching game. Download now to conquer every sorting challenge and be the master of Color Sorting!
