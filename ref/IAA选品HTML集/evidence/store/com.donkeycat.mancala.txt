About this game
arrow_forward
Play Mancala with friends online or offline. The board game is now available with online multiplayer. You can also play offline against challenging computer opponents or in the two player mode.
Mancala is a simple but demanding puzzle strategy game where you try to move stones in your Mangala and capture opponent's stones to win the game.
Online multiplayer 👥
Play a quick Mancala game online against players all around the world. No login is necessary. Send your opponents emojis during the game.
Offline multiplayer 🆚
Play Mancala and friends on one device with the tow player mode.
Computer opponents 👤🤖
If you don't want to play Mangala with friends, try to improve your strategy and practice against the computer in 2 player mode. Play against three different computer opponents offline.
Test your skills against three different computer opponents.
Leaderboard 🏆
Compare your sills and your game statistics with other players. Get a detailed overview of your skill development.
Community
Join the Ayo Online community and play Mangala with Friends.
Start right away
No login necessary, play Mancala with friends right away.
Classic Board Game 🎲
Mancala is known around the world and has lots of different names like Mangala, Ayo, Mankalah, Manqala, Mankala, Sungka, Congklak , Magala, Macala
Mancala is a fast strategy game that is easy to learn and offers both beginners and experienced players strategic challenges for your brain
If you're an advanced player already, try to win against the best players online!
If you are a beginner and don't have a strategy yet. You can start with training against the Computer or in the offline 2 player mode before you enter the challenging world of Mancala. 😉

I am responding to the previous comment made by Gerardo. There seems to be some misunderstanding here about the game. Mancala, at least the version of the game played here (and there are many) is a "perfect game" in the sense that the player who moves first and makes the best move on each turn, will always win. As such, in practical play, the player who moves first has a huge advantage. For example, I just played a player ranked 2000 ELO points higher than me and easily beat that player because I was able to move first. All of the top players know this and many of the lower ranked players suspect this. The top players want to protect their ELO rating and will purposely avoid playing lower ranked players out of fear of losing rating points if they do not get to move first. And even if they do get to move first, the higher rated player will only get a few ELO points when beating a much lower ranked player. So there is not much incentive for them to play lower ranked players. Since this mancala app allows you to choose your opponent, there is a lot of declines on invites made in the game for the reasons stated above. Another more popular mancala game in the Play Store does not allow the player to choose his opponent and does not allow a player to decline to play without loosing the game. So in that app, there is much more games taking place than in this app. My suggestion to the app developer is to use the same format for this app so there is more people playing and less people declining to play.
