About this game
arrow_forward
Happy Color® is the ultimate color by number zen experience, loved by millions of users worldwide! Discover over 40,000 FREE high‑quality coloring pages in one app. From nature, animals, and mandalas to unique Disney and Marvel scenes and original art coloring, there’s something for everyone.
Whether you’re looking for zen and relaxation, stress relief, or art therapy, adult coloring with Happy Color® is the perfect way to unwind. Just tap to paint by numbers and watch your drawings come to life – anytime, anywhere.
Reasons to download Happy Color®
FIND PICTURES YOU LOVE
Happy Color® now includes a powerful search feature that can help you explore the world of paint by number. Search, discover, and color the pictures that matter to you, whether they relate to your hobbies, passions, zen, or favorite topics. That’s what makes our coloring book so special—everyone can find joy in coloring what they love most.
ICONIC MARVEL HEROES
Marvel has officially joined Happy Color, and your next coloring adventure starts now! Discover iconic heroes, dive into spectacular artwork, and explore a universe that has inspired generations of fans around the world. Start exploring the pictures today – and don’t put your cape away just yet. More Marvel pictures are already on the way!
UNIQUE DISNEY CONTENT
Happy Color® collaborates with world-renowned studios to bring you unique content. Rediscover unforgettable moments and color some of your favorite characters in iconic scenes from Star Wars, Disney's Beauty and the Beast, The Lion King, Aladdin, Cinderella, Winnie the Pooh, and many more in Happy Color®.
COLOR ANYWHERE, ANYTIME
Enjoy coloring for adults at home, during a break, or on the go. Whether you want a moment of zen, need a little stress relief, or just love paint by number art, Happy Color® is always ready.
COLOR FOR A GOOD CAUSE
Happy Color® proudly supports global charitable initiatives. Through our special color by number charity events, you can color for a purpose: learn about meaningful causes, enjoy unique drawing collections, and make an impact. You color, we donate.
ART CREATED BY PROFESSIONAL ARTISTS
More than 100 talented artists from all over the world create exclusive art coloring pages for Happy Color®. Each drawing in our coloring book for adults is hand‑crafted with care, from detailed mandalas to soothing nature scenes. Enjoy art therapy at its best while exploring unique, inspiring styles that help you relax and recharge.
FREE FOR EVERYONE
All 40,000+ paint by number pictures are absolutely FREE. We believe that adult coloring should be accessible to everyone – quality coloring books shouldn’t come at a cost.
Happy Color® is special. It is not just a color by number app – it’s a global community of people who love art, zen, drawing, and coloring for adults. Our users find zen, creativity, and positivity in their everyday lives through art therapy. Every tap in this paint by number app is a small moment of joy.
This coloring book for adults combines creativity, mindfulness, and paint by number fun into one app. Whether you’re into detailed art coloring, want to improve your drawing focus, or simply need relaxing art therapy, Happy Color® is here to bring you zen and joy – picture by picture.
Made with love and loved by millions of users worldwide
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
"I absolutely LOVE this app! If I could give it 100 stars, I would. It’s so much fun to color, and unlike other color‑by‑number apps, this one has a massive art library with new pictures added every day. I adore the variety – nature, birds, butterflies, art, and the mystery pictures are my favorite. I’m a lifelong fan now!" (c)
