Bead 16 - 16 Guti Game
, a classic Indian two-player abstract strategy board game also known as 16 Goti, Sholo Guti, and Sixteen Soldiers. Jump over your opponent's pieces, capture all 16 pieces, and win the game.
16 Guti is a famous and popular traditional game in South-East Asia. It is also enjoyed in other parts of the world because it is similar to classic board games like Chess and Checkers or Draughts. In
Sixteen Soldiers
, the player who captures all 16 of the opponent's pieces wins.In India, this game is known as 16 Goti, 16 Kaati Game, and Indian Checkers.
Bead 12 (Baro Guti) is a traditional and popular variant of the Bead 16 game. It is known by different names in different countries. In Bangladesh, it is known as Baro Guti, while in India, it is known as Bara Tehni.
Our free Align It - Sixteen Soldiers game offers:
-
Single-player game
(play against the CPU)
- Play two games: Bead 16 and Bead 12 (12 Tehni)
- Three difficulty levels in single-player mode
-
Online game
(play and chat with friends and other players from around the world)
- Two-player game (
Multiplayer Game
)
- Multiple themes and game boards
- Unlock and collect achievement badges
- Compete in weekly and monthly leagues
- Detailed game statistics
Enjoy 16 Goti, Sholo Guti, and Baro Guti with precise controls, smooth gameplay, and attractive graphics. The first move and the color of your pieces are decided by a toss. In multiplayer mode, you can challenge and play with friends and family members.
Damru Game is a great way to enjoy your free time. Indian Checkers is a popular multiplayer strategy game. If you are already a pro, switch to online mode and challenge players in 16 Goti Game, or play against the computer to train your brain and improve your strategy.
