About this game
arrow_forward
🎹Piano Fire is a classic piano tile game featuring various music genres, offering you a fantastic gameplay experience.
😉Whether you are a hardcore player or just want to relax while listening to your favorite songs, online or offline, this game will satisfy you all the same.
🎵Attention🎵
We add new songs to the game every month! If you have any suggestions, please leave a comment or email us at adaricmusic@gmail.com.
How to Play:
Tiles will fall from above. When they reach the line at the bottom, tap them to earn "Melody Points".🎼
Some tiles may fall fast, but don't worry—every single tile's timing is carefully designed by our professional music team. Tap to the beat of music, and you'll make it!😎
Game Features:
🌐Global Ranking – Compete with players worldwide.
📲Local Music – Import songs from your phone.
📅Monthly Events – Play songs to collect tokens and exchange them for game items.
🔥Easy to Play, Hard to Master – Try our expert-level songs; collecting all three stars will take some time!
Once you've played a song, you won't need an internet connection🚫🛜to play that song again—it will be downloaded automatically📲. Then you will be able to play it anytime you want, whether to relieve stress or sharpen your reaction—this game will be a good choice!
If any music producers or labels have concerns about the music or images used in the game, or if players have suggestions for improvement, please feel free to contact us at adaricmusic@gmail.com.
