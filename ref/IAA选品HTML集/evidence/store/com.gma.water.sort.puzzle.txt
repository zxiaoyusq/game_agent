About this game
arrow_forward
Water Sort Puzzle is a fun and interesting puzzle game! Try to sort the colored water in the glasses until all colors are in the same glass. A challenging yet relaxing game to exercise your brain!
★ HOW TO PLAY:
• Tap any glass to pour water to another glass.
• The rule is that you can only pour the water if it is linked to the same color and there’s enough space on the glass.
• Try not to get stuck - but don’t worry, you can always restart the level at any time.
★ FEATURES:
• One finger control.
• Multiple unique levels
• FREE & EASY TO PLAY.
• NO penalties & time limits; you can enjoy Water Sort Puzzle at your own pace!
