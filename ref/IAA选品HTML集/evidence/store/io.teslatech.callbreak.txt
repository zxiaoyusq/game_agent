About this game
arrow_forward
Welcome to Callbreak, the classic trick-taking card game loved across India! Whether you call it Lakadi, Lakdi, or taash game, this strategic 4-player game delivers non-stop entertainment with smart AI and online multiplayer.
If you enjoy popular trick-taking card games like Spades, Call Bridge, or 29 card game, or love playing card games like Teen Patti (3 patti) and Indian Rummy, Callbreak offers the ultimate test of bidding skill and taash strategy!
KEY FEATURES & GAME MODES
🔸Online Multiplayer: Compete with real players across India and across the World and climb the global leaderboards.
🔸Private Tables: Create private rooms to play Call break game with friends and family.
🔸Smart AI Bots: Practice playing cards against adaptive AI opponents anytime in this taash game.
🔸Custom Themes: Personalize your table with beautiful card decks and rich wallpapers.
🔸Speed Control & Autoplay: Adjust card game speed from fast to relaxed, or toggle autoplay when needed.
🔸Interactive Tutorial: Learn how to play Callbreak and master bidding strategy in under 2 minutes.
HOW TO PLAY CALLBREAK (LAKDI)
Callbreak is played with a standard 52-card deck among 4 players (13 cards each). Spades are always the trump suit:
🔸The Deal: All 52 cards are dealt equally (13 cards per player).
🔸Bidding (Call): Players make a bid ("Call") from 1 to 13 tricks based on hand strength.
🔸Trick Play: Players must follow the led suit if possible. If out of suit, play a Spade (trump) or any other card.
🔸Scoring: Reach or exceed your bid to score positive points. Winning fewer tricks than your bid results in a point penalty!
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
🔸What is Callbreak?
Callbreak (also known as Call break, Lakdi, Lakadi, or Callbridge) is a strategic trick-taking card game popular in India and Nepal. It is widely searched as ताश गेम (tash game), tash wala game, or लकड़ी गेम.
🔸Can I play Callbreak offline?
Yes! This offline card game works completely without internet or wifi. Play against intelligent AI opponents whenever you are on the go.
🔸Is Callbreak free to play?
Yes, this card game is 100% free to download and play, with customizable features and daily play rewards.
