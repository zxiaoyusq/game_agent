About this game
arrow_forward
Classic piano games just got fine-tuned! Play the original piano game Piano Tiles 2™ and tap to play your favorite tunes!
Test your rhythmic reaction skills in addicting, challenging levels, get cool bonuses and try limited-time events.
Tap musical tiles to play piano songs in this modern upgrade to the classic piano game. Make piano music like magic when you tap the black tiles, progressing through levels. But be careful, DON'T TOUCH THE WHITE TILES!
Tap to the beat with 1.1 BILLION players around the world! Download Piano Tiles 2™ today!
=== Piano Tiles 2™ features ===
🎹
PIANO MUSIC GAME
🎹
- Tap along to over 700+ songs!
- Popular classical piano tunes you know and love
- Folk music favorites from around the world
- Original tracks from other players (send us yours!)
- Fire new hits with music added often!
🎵
EVENTS & CHALLENGES
🎵
- Tap through bonus challenges & become a star!
- Earn rewards for completing daily missions & events!
- Leaderboard challenges: see how you stack up worldwide & climb to the top!
- Key in your coordination with accompaniments, double tiles, sliders & more!
🎼
MUSICAL GAME MODES
🎶
- Play piano with progressively challenging levels: are you Chopsticks or Chopin?
- Classic Piano Tiles gameplay for retro game lovers
- New modes like slider, combo tile & double tile
- Speed modes for beginners, skilled players, and masters
- Level up to unlock new tracks & gameplay
- Casual game with challenging modes- keep up with the keys!
⚡
PIANO POWER-UPS & MORE
⚡
- Power-up your game with boosters!
- Protect white tile taps, freeze time & more!
- Jazz out too hard and tap the wrong tile? Use a revive!
- Lucky Spins for more props, new tracks & more rewards!
🌐
PLAY ONLINE OR OFFLINE
🚫
- Play online & compete on the leaderboards
- Play offline, no WIFI
- Tap the black tiles anytime, anywhere. No internet or grand piano required!
Addicting game to test your reaction and musical mettle, tap to the beat & become a pop piano sensation! A Reddot game design award winner, Piano Tiles 2™ has sleek graphics & tons of tracks to try. Casual yet engaging, relaxing yet challenging, Piano Tiles 2™ has tons of music and gameplay modes for anyone to enjoy. Tap to download now!
We are not able to support Facebook Login save files because save files from the previous developer are no longer accessible. Please contact customer support with any questions, and we will work to accommodate you as best as we can.
