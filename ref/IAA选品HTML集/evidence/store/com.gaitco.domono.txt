About this game
arrow_forward
Domono is the #1 Egyptian dominoes game for your phone! Challenge friends and rivals from
everywhere in the most fun and fastest dominoes experience, with a polished design and
smooth play.
Game modes:
• Online: quick matches against real players from around the world.
• Offline: practice against bots at multiple difficulty levels, anytime, anywhere.
• With friends: play over your local network (LAN) or invite friends with a table code.
• Solo & teams: 1v1 matches, or 2v2 team play with your partner.
Features:
• Tables & clubs: build your own club and play with your community.
• Tournaments: compete in knockout brackets and climb to the top.
• Ranks: rise from Bronze all the way to Diamond through a fair rating system.
• Leaderboards: compare your results with the best players daily and weekly.
• Daily missions & achievements: fresh rewards every day to keep you on top.
• Voice chat: talk with teammates and opponents during the match.
• Themes & cosmetics: customize your table and dominoes in style.
