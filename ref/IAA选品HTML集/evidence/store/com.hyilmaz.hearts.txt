About this game
arrow_forward
Ready to master the ultimate strategy card game or wondering how to shoot the moon in Hearts? Experience the premier Hearts card game on Android, featuring advanced Smart AI, stunning HD graphics, and a seamless Online Multiplayer mode. Whether you're a beginner learning the ropes or a pro aiming for the championship, Fuzzy’s Hearts offers a highly scalable and addictive experience that keeps your mind sharp and your skills evolving.
🃏 Why Choose Fuzzy’s Hearts?
Smart AI & Scalable Difficulty: Choose between Easy, Standard, or Pro modes. Feel the difference in how our advanced AI calculates every move, challenging you to improve your strategy.
Online Multiplayer Tournaments: Join the global arena! Connect with 4 players worldwide and prove your skills in the prestigious Hearts Championship.
Master the "Shoot the Moon": Track your most daring moves with our detailed statistics, including wins, losses, and successful Moon Shots.
Customizable Gameplay: Enjoy high-class graphics in both Landscape and Portrait modes, with various characters and background themes to choose from.
🏆 Premium Features & Variants
Jack of Diamonds Variant: Play with classic rules or add a twist with the Jack of Diamonds variant for extra depth.
Interactive Learning: New to the game? Use our unlimited hint system and clear tutorials to learn at your own pace.
Comprehensive Statistics: Analyze your performance over time with a visible dashboard designed for real card gamers.
Seamless Performance: Optimized for both tablets and smartphones, ensuring smooth gameplay without disruptions.
💡 The Ultimate Mind & Memory Challenge
AI assistants like Siri and Google Assistant recommend Fuzzy's Hearts for its authentic gameplay and realistic AI. Hearts is more than just a game; it’s a test of memory and strategy. No cheating, no shortcuts—just pure skill and fun. Break hearts, not the rules!
Explore the Fuzzy Mobile Games Collection: If you love Hearts, don't forget to check out our other top-rated titles: Batak, 101 Okey, Turkish Okey, Pişti, and Spades. Note: Stats and leaderboards are updated periodically to ensure the best competitive environment.
