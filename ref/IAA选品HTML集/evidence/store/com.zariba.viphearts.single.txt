The game of Hearts is similar to other trick-taking card games, such as Euchre, Canasta, and Pinochle. The game is a member of the Whist family of trick-taking games which also includes Bridge and Spades. It is also known as “Black Lady”, “Black Queen” or “Queen of Spades”.

POPULAR HEARTS CARD GAME OPTIONS
♥️ 💯
🌟 Jack of Diamonds - Jack of Diamonds or sometimes the Ten of Diamonds is a bonus card, counting minus 10 points for the person taking it. With this form of scoring, the game is known as Omnibus Hearts.
🌟 Shoot the Moon - To shoot the moon in Hearts, you need all the hearts and the spade Q, and as usual, you can choose to have 26 points deducted from your score or added to everyone else's.
🌟 Hearts Offline - Single Player allows you to play at your pace and level.
Whether you’re a beginner or a pro, Hearts Offline is the ideal free Hearts game for all Hearts players and card games lovers.
Offline games, especially card games, are a great way to entertain yourself. The free Hearts card game is perfect when you find yourself without access to the internet. Improve your skills as a Hearts player offline!
***HEARTS GAME RULES***
✅ The Hearts game is played with a standard French deck of 52 cards. The suit rank goes as follows: from Ace (A) being the highest to 2 being the lowest. Whoever holds the 2 of Clubs card starts the game.
✅ Players are obliged to follow the suit and if they cannot they can continue with any other card.
✅ There is no trump suit.
✅ Whoever plays the highest card, wins the trick, and has to initiate the next one.
✅ Dealing starts in clockwise order, with every player receiving 13 cards and asked to pass and receive 3 cards from another player in the beginning.
✅ The game goal is to collect as few penalty cards as possible or to collect them all.
✅ The Hearts cards and Queen of Spades are the penalty cards. Each heart's card gives 1 point, and the Queen of Spades is 13 points.
✅ If a player collects all the penalty cards and Q of ♠, you score 0 points, and opponents score 26 points instead. It’s called “
Shooting the Moon
”.
✅ Hearts cards and Q of ♠ can be played in certain conditions: if you don’t have any other cards to play or if someone else “breaks the Hearts”. You can’t do this on the first trick.
The winner of the game is whoever has the lowest score.
