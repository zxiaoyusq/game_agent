About this game
arrow_forward
Plan every move in FreeCell Solitaire, the classic single-player card game built around strategy and open information. Arrange cards in descending order with alternating colors, use four free cells to create space, and move every suit to the foundations from Ace to King.
CLASSIC FREECELL, MADE FOR MOBILE
• Deal all 52 cards face up across eight tableau columns.
• Move cards and valid sequences onto the next lower rank in the opposite color.
• Hold one card in each free cell while you reorganize the tableau.
• Build four foundation piles by suit from Ace through King.
PLAY OFFLINE AT YOUR OWN PACE
Enjoy a focused card game anytime without a network connection. Your unfinished game is saved automatically, so you can pause and return when it suits you. Choose random or numbered deals for a fresh puzzle or a specific setup.
HELPFUL CONTROLS AND TOOLS
Use unlimited undo and hints when you want to reconsider a move. Card highlighting, tap-to-move controls, automatic moves, and clear card sizes keep the board easy to manage. Switch between portrait and landscape orientation to match your screen.
MAKE THE TABLE YOURS
Choose from multiple card styles and backgrounds, adjust the game settings, and enjoy a victory animation after a successful deal. Detailed statistics and a timer help you follow your FreeCell history and compare sessions.
Start your next FreeCell deal and find the sequence that opens the board.
Play classic FreeCell offline with hints, unlimited undo, and clear cards.
