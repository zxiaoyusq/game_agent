About this game
arrow_forward
Arrow Puzzle is a single-player logic game that turns simple taps into a rewarding challenge. Your goal is straightforward: find a free path, tap arrows in the right order, and enjoy as the maze slowly turns into a clear, empty board on every level. Follow the arrows to escape the puzzle and find a soothing flow that keeps your mind engaged and relaxed.
Looking for a gentle workout for your brain without any pressure attached? This comforting single-player arrow game lets you tap away the stress, gives you a focused break from the noise, and helps you train your logic and spatial thinking as you build a calming daily ritual. Inspired by abstract graphic games, each cleared board in the arrow game is a small mental reset—a satisfying win for your mind.
How to Play Arrow Puzzle:
• In this tap away puzzle game, your goal is to clear the grid—a maze of arrows all pointing in different directions.
• Escape the arrow puzzle grid by finding a free path. An arrow moves only when its way ahead is clear and no other arrows are blocking its path. Take a moment to think first.
• Tap arrows away one by one to help them leave the grid and open up more space for the others. Clean visuals inspired by abstract graphic games help you focus on every move.
• Avoid blocked arrows. If you tap an arrow whose path is blocked, you lose a life. Lives are limited on each level, but you can always rethink your plan and try again at your own pace.
• If you get stuck, use helpful hints to highlight an arrow that can safely escape the maze.
• Plan the order of your taps, help every arrow escape, and master this abstract graphic puzzle game one level at a time.
Why You'll Love Arrow Puzzle:
✔ A minimalist arrow game with simple rules and deep strategy—easy to start, satisfying to master.
✔ Hundreds of handcrafted tap away style puzzles that grow in challenge as you play.
✔ Daily Challenges—play every day, complete monthly challenges, and collect unique trophies.
✔ Helpful hints when you need a small nudge to release an arrow and keep the flow going.
✔ No timer, no pressure—a relaxing escape and a true delight for your mind.
✔ Clean, calming visuals and smooth animations that keep the focus on puzzle-solving in this single-player tap game.
Play Arrow Puzzle, clear the grid of arrows, and enjoy a rare balance of sharp focus and deep relaxation.
